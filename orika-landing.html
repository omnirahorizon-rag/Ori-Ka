Sequential Verification

The Phase Architecture

The 75-day cycle applies controlled pressure across three sequential phases. Each phase answers a distinct question. No phase may be skipped, and no element may be optimized before it has been proven stable.

I
Days 01 — 25
Acclimation
Compliance Phase — System Entry & Stabilization

Establishes a singular truth: Can the practitioner follow the architecture exactly as designed? Objective: elimination of optimization. Total compliance and stabilization of behavior under rigid structure. Any attempt at "personalization" or schedule modification is classified as non-compliance and results in an immediate reset.

If you cannot follow structure, you cannot build. Those who pass do not talk about change; they have already begun it.
II
Days 26 — 50
Resistance
Stress Phase — Failure Identification & Correction

Identifies what breaks when controlled pressure is applied. The forensic analysis of instability. Pressure introduced through increased workload density, time constraints, and systematic reduction of external reminders. Every missed action and emotional reaction must be recorded. Concealment of failure is a terminal breach.

Pressure does not create weakness. It reveals it. If you break and correct it, you become stable.
III
Days 51 — 75
Refinement
Optimization Phase — Verified Stability Upgrade

Answers the question: What is stable enough to be improved? This phase does not fix weakness; it advances strength. No element may be optimized unless it has survived Phase II without a breakdown pattern. One variable is adjusted at a time. If instability appears, the system reverts to Phase II baseline.

Refinement is not creativity; it is precision applied to proven systems. If you refine too early, you destroy what was not stable.
